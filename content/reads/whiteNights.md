<u>GoodReads Overview:</u> White Nights is a short story by Fyodor Dostoevsky that was published in 1848. Set in St. Petersburg, it is the story of a young man fighting his inner restlessness. A light and tender narrative, it delves into the torment and guilt of unrequited love. Both protagonists suffer from a deep sense of alienation that initially brings them together. A blend of romanticism and realism, the story appeals gently to the senses and feelings.

<u>Mode:</u> Audiobook.

<u>My Thoughts:</u> I picked this book up because I wanted something to listen to while I crochet my new project. It was a decent book. The dreamer's character was quite interesting, the intense yapping in second night, the dramatic monologues, but the book was a bit of a bore to me.

I failed to find it as profound as others did, I could not quite sympathize with the dreamer's loneliness, which he brought upon himself. If only he redeems his life by accepting the invitation. The idiocy of both Nastenka and our dreamer here, was beyond measurable.
