<u>GoodReads Overview:</u> While in Paris on business, Harvard symbologist Robert Langdon receives an urgent late-night phone call: the elderly curator of the Louvre has been murdered inside the museum. Near the body, police have found a baffling cipher. While working to solve the enigmatic riddle, Langdon is stunned to discover it leads to a trail of clues hidden in the works of Da Vinci - clues visible for all to see - yet ingeniously disguised by the painter.

<u>Mode:</u> Online, ebook.

<u>My Thoughts:</u> What even was this book? The start got me good, it sounded like a perfect thriller novel, but oh boy. How everything went downhill, I expected this book to be well-researched, but the plot was all over the place, the characters shallow, I did not care about the Grail, and I surely did not learn any history. I have no more words for this book. Yes, it was a "fictional" book but fiction can also be written nicely, maybe this is very subjective, but I did not enjoy the book.
